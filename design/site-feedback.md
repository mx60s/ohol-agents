# Site: user feedback on design/site-home.html (2026-11-16)

Annotated screenshot: `design/feedback/site-home-1366-markup.png`. Stream equivalent: `stream-feedback.md`.

## General rules (apply to every site page: home, people, agent, families, study)

- **Remove ~90% of the small eyebrow/caption over-explanations.** Title-bar right-side captions ("this run · since 14:02", "click anyone for their page", "every life, in trait space", "living people · 140 × 112 tiles", "14 total", "narrator · every 30 s"), sub-captions under stat numbers ("4 adults · 3 children · 2 babies", "+4 Eves", "9 starvation · 5 cold", "n = 14 · IQR 0.5–16", "age ≥ 14, of those born", ...), explainer boxes ("Reading the people: ..."), and footer jokes. Keep a caption only where a number would be misread without it. On the study page, keep n counts and uncertainty notes on charts (that's research hygiene, not over-explanation), but cut the prose around them hard.
- **Don't over-delineate families.** Families share a last name, which is enough. Drop the per-family colour dots and marker shapes on people cards, death rows and in legends, and drop the family legends. Family colour may stay only where it carries data a reader can't get otherwise (e.g. points in the personality cloud), with no shape coding and no separate legend.

## Specific removals on the home page

- Status strip: the run id ("Run 2026-11-16-a"), the word "seats", and the whole "Trait readout: MOCK random, not from neurons yet" item.
- World status: the title-bar caption, every sub-caption under the stat numbers, and the whole LLM SPEND tile.
- The people: "click anyone for their page", the family legend (HOPE / ASHGROVE / MOSS), the "on stream" badge, and the "cold" chip on Eve Moss's card (keep only urgent chips like starving, or make them consistent).
- Map: the "living people · 140 × 112 tiles" caption.
- Personality cloud: the "every life, in trait space" caption and the "Reading the people" explainer box.
- Recent deaths: "14 total".
- World news: "narrator · every 30 s".
- Footer: "TRAITS SHOWN ARE MOCK. INDIVIDUAL RESULTS VARY."

## MOCK labelling

Keep it minimal: one small MOCK badge where trait numbers are shown (the personality cloud, the agent page's personality panel, study charts). No repeated disclaimers.
