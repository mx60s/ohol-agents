How to read Q1

What would count

  • A slope whose 95% band excludes 0, pooled across runs, after controlling for mother's food at birth.
  • Survival model: Cox, alive lives censored.

Confounds

  • Babies mostly die of neglect, which is about the mother's traits, not their own.
  • Lineages share trait centres, so family and trait are tangled.
Right now: no pattern you should believe. 9 starvations, 5 cold.
